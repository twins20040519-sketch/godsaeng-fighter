2. Purpose and target audience
GODSAENG FIGHTER helps people build daily habits ("godsaeng" is Korean slang for living a productive, well-organized day). Users record four daily habits: wake-up time, pages read, workout minutes, and study minutes. These records become the power of their pixel-art fighter, and they compete in arcade-style battles with friends or other users doing the same habit. The target audience is Korean students and young adults (14+) who want motivation to keep healthy routines. The value is turning self-improvement into a fun, social game with streaks and collectible characters.

3. How to access the main features
No login credentials are required. On first launch the app creates an anonymous account automatically and only asks for a nickname.
- Today tab ("오늘"): tap any category card (e.g. workout "헬창").
- Choose a battle mode: "랜덤 1:1" (random 1:1), "10인 난투" (11-player battle) or "친구방" (friends room). Empty seats in random battles are filled with CPU opponents, so every feature can be tested with a single device.
- Proof tab ("인증"): record the habit. Workout = start/stop timer plus one photo. Study = live camera timer. Reading = enter book title, total pages, and current page. Wake-up = record time.
- Battle tab ("배틀"): watch the animated battle based on today's records.
- Results tab ("결과") and My tab ("마이"): points, coins, streaks, calendar, history.
- Report/Block: in any online room, tap "신고" (report) or "차단" (block) next to another user's nickname. Blocked users' nicknames and characters are hidden immediately. Reports are stored on our server and reviewed by the developer.
- Account deletion: My tab > 계정 > 계정 삭제 deletes the account and all records from the server.

User-generated content: the only user-generated content is the nickname. There is no chat or free-text sharing. Nicknames are limited to letters/numbers and are filtered for offensive words, and users can report or block other users as described above. Workout photos and the study camera view stay on the device and are never uploaded or shown to others.

4. External services
- Supabase (supabase.com): anonymous authentication and database for nicknames, battle rooms, and daily records.
- Netlify (netlify.com): hosts the privacy policy and support pages.
No payment processors, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or AI services are used. The app has no in-app purchases; coins are earned only through gameplay.

5. Regional differences
The app functions the same in all regions. The interface is in Korean.

Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party content. All characters and artwork were created for this app.
